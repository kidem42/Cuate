Cuate · Brand Sheet

Cuate
KWAH-teh · from Nahuatl cōātl — "twin"
Press a key anywhere. Your twin shows up.

The mark — el eclipse

One disc, two lights. Warm from below, cold from above. That is the twins: you and it are the same body, lit from two sides. The shape is inherited from the previous sphere icon, so the transition doesn't read as a break.

Geometry

Disc A: r = 300. The warm twin is a copy of A offset by (−28, −106), with a crescent thickness of 110. The cold one is offset by (+18, +68), thickness 70. Everything derives from a single radius, so the mark can be rebuilt at any size without the source file.

Rules
✓The warm crescent is always at the bottomThe light comes from below — as it did in the original icon, and still does
✓The clear space is half the disc's radiusNothing closer than 0.5r to the mark's edge
✓The minimum size is 16 pxBelow that we switch to the monochrome menu-bar glyph
✗Never flip or mirror itAn inverted mark reads as a waning moon
✗Never recolor the crescents with theme colorsThe eight themes paint the interface, not the brand
✗Never add an outline, a shadow or text inside the mark

The menu-bar glyph

A template image: alpha only, with the system assigning the color. The crescent is thickened to 0.45r — at 18 pt a thin crescent falls apart. State is shown with geometry, not color: the twin moves closer and the crescent narrows.

Color

The palette was eyedropped from the original icon — it isn't a new set but the same light, written down as numbers. The two accents are the twins: warm and cold.

Proportion

Roughly 80 % obsidiana, 12 % neutral surfaces, 6 % ámbar, 2 % cielo. Ámbar is rare and therefore noticeable. Too much of it and the product starts to look like a warning.

Typeface

Interface and site
Cuate

A geometric grotesque with humanist shapes. In the app it is the system face (SF Pro / Roboto). On the site, Inter or Geist. No letterspacing in the logo, no italics, no all caps.

Code and keys
⌘⇧Space

Monospaced — SF Mono or JetBrains Mono. Hotkeys are always set in key symbols, never in words: ⌥Space, not "Option+Space".

The ua pair is the word's visual signature. Never tighten it with kerning.

Voice

Cuate is neither a servant nor a boss. It is an equal. Everything else follows from that: short sentences, no apologies, no enthusiasm.

Do
✓"Done. Copied to the clipboard."
✓"No Mistral key found. Add one?"
✓"The model isn't in memory. Loading will take about a minute."
✓In Spanish, tú"¿Le echamos un ojo?", never "usted"
Don't
✗"Great! I successfully copied the text! 🎉"
✗"Sorry, I was unable to find your API key…"
✗"Your personal AI assistant is ready to help"
✗A name for the AI itselfIt IS Cuate. It has no second name

Writing rules

RuleExample
No exclamation marks in the interface"File saved."
An error = what happened + what to do"The provider returned 429. Wait or switch models."
No emoji in the product or in releases—
The word "AI" belongs in descriptors, not in the product's speech"Cuate — AI on any screen", but inside it is just "Cuate"
The name is never inflected in any language"Open Cuate", never a declined form of it

The site

The first screen

An obsidiana background, the mark, the name, a single line of descriptor and a live screen recording: the hotkey press — the panel — the answer. No screenshot collages and no people in headphones.

What to show

The product inside someone else's window — over mail, code, a spreadsheet. The value is that it comes to you rather than you to it; an interface in a vacuum doesn't show that.

The app's themes (blueprint, terminal, synthwave, sakura, halloween, Día de Muertos) are a showcase, not an identity. They can and should be shown, but as a separate block, not as the site's primary language.
